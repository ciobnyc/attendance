Shows how strongly each topic's favorability score tracks with a school's CA rate.

Topic Correlation p-value Significant?
How to read this: The correlation number ranges from -1 to 1. A negative number means schools that score higher on that topic tend to have lower CA rates, the pattern we'd expect if the topic matters for attendance. The closer the number is to -1, the stronger that pattern. The p-value tells us how likely it is that this pattern is just chance rather than something real. Green means the pattern is statistically significant (very unlikely to be chance). Amber means it's borderline. Gray means there isn't a clear pattern. Groups under about 15 schools (6 to 12, Transfer, K to 12) should be read as suggestive, not conclusive, since there's so little data to work with.
